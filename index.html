How it works

Teams that form around a problem stick together.

  1. 01

    Founders post the problem

    Describe what you're solving, how far along it is, and the roles you need. Problem first, pitch second.

  2. 02

    Builders say why it matters

    Instead of a resume, builders ask to join by explaining why this problem matters to them.

  3. 03

    Accept, and we introduce you

    When a founder accepts, CoBldr emails you both so the conversation starts right away.
